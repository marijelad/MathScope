Triangle Geometry

Understand the idea, see why it works, follow a complete example, then test your understanding.

1. The core idea

Mathematics becomes reliable when each step follows from a property that remains true. In this lesson, the central relationship is:

A + B + C = 180°
Why this works

A line through one vertex parallel to the opposite side creates alternate interior angles. Those three angles form a straight line, proving that the interior angles of a Euclidean triangle total 180°.

2. Worked example

Follow the reasoning one step at a time. Each transformation keeps the mathematical meaning intact.

Known angles
A = 48°, B = 67°
Use the angle sum
48 + 67 + C = 180
Combine
115 + C = 180
Solve
C = 65°
