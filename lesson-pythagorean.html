Pythagorean Theorem

Understand the idea, see why it works, follow a complete example, then test your understanding.

1. The core idea

Mathematics becomes reliable when each step follows from a property that remains true. In this lesson, the central relationship is:

a² + b² = c²
Why this works

For a right triangle, areas of squares built on the two legs together equal the area of the square on the hypotenuse. Rearrangement proofs show this without relying on numerical examples.

2. Worked example

Follow the reasoning one step at a time. Each transformation keeps the mathematical meaning intact.

Legs
a = 6, b = 8
Substitute
6² + 8² = c²
Simplify
36 + 64 = 100
Square root
c = 10
