Trigonometric Functions

Understand the idea, see why it works, follow a complete example, then test your understanding.

1. The core idea

Mathematics becomes reliable when each step follows from a property that remains true. In this lesson, the central relationship is:

y = sin x
Why this works

On the unit circle, sine is the vertical coordinate of a rotating point. Repeated rotations repeat the coordinate values, producing periodic wave behavior.

2. Worked example

Follow the reasoning one step at a time. Each transformation keeps the mathematical meaning intact.

Unit circle angle
θ = 30°
Sine is y-coordinate
sin 30° = 1/2
Period
sin(θ+360°)=sin θ
Amplitude
Basic sine amplitude = 1
