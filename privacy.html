Privacy by design

A simple privacy note for this portfolio demonstration.

Local progress data

MathScope uses your browser's localStorage to remember which demo lessons you mark complete. This data remains on your device unless you clear it.

Contact form

The included contact form is a front-end demonstration and does not submit information to a server.

Analytics and cookies

No analytics service, advertising tracker or third-party cookie is included in this version.

Production deployment

If this project is adapted for a real service, the privacy notice should be updated to describe the actual hosting, analytics, authentication and data-processing systems used.
